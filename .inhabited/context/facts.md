# Facts allowed on the page (all taken from the live sistudio.app on 2026-11-16)

Plans
- Both plans start with a 7-day free trial.
- S.I.S. Basic, RM 199 / year: print-ready PDF invoices; upload old files and let AI read client details (usage allowance applies); sales, expenses, net profit and business overview; clients, rates, briefs, jobs and records; reports and CSV export.
- S.I.S. Pro, RM 399 / year: everything in Basic; in-app studio AI for invoices and client records; connect your own AI (Claude, Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, or any MCP-compatible assistant) through API / MCP; manual Google Drive backup from an invoice page; priority WhatsApp support and early access to new tools.

Product
- Upload past invoices, receipts, PDFs or photos (PDF, JPG, PNG), up to 50 per batch. AI reads the client details. Nothing is saved until the owner reviews and approves. AI never edits records directly.
- Save clients, rates, bank details and payment terms once; reuse them on the next invoice. Choose a client and a service from the rate card, then generate the invoice and its PDF from the same record.
- A client page holds company details, contacts and past invoices. Company and individual clients are separate.
- Reports: sales, expenses and net profit by month; client invoice summaries and ranking; CSV export for an accountant.
- Pro AI connection: authorised studio members create each connection token and can revoke it at any time; the AI only works within the permitted studio scope.

Trust (from /trust)
- Data (clients, invoices, payments, uploaded receipts) is stored in Supabase's Singapore region. The site runs on Vercel's Singapore edge.
- Each studio's data is isolated at the database level with row-level security.
- Data is not sold and not handed to anyone for marketing.
- Run by Madvengers Production, Malaysian business registration 003357155-P.

Support (from /contact)
