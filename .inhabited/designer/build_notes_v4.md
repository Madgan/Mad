## 1. What changed, item by item

### 1. Ticks on a clock (`story()`, `landed()`, `receipts()`)

- **The PDF's receipt.** `storyBeat()` no longer returns a tick; nothing about the receipt reads the scroll position. `landed()` is the finish callback of the 177 ms drop animation: on that frame `tick=1` (`data-state="sent"`: one grey tick, "已发出 / Sent"), and two timers are set, `TICK=260` ms and 520 ms, for `delivered` and `read`. Each is a cut of `data-state`. The `file` state is gone. Going back above the drop threshold calls `tickStop()`, sets `tick=0`, and the bubble is `empty` again whatever it had reached; coming down again plays the drop and the clock again. Where the drop is not played (reduced motion, `BOOT`, no Web Animations, a page opened below the drop) `tick=3`; without script the markup's `data-state="read"` stands.
- **Measured** (`tools/rb3_story.js`, a per-frame log in the page while wheeling; `tools/rb3_capture_log.txt`, "the receipt clock: per-frame log"): at 300, 600 and 1,500 px a second at 390 x 844 and 800 at 1280 x 800: sent at 0, delivered at 266, read at 534 ms after the landing frame in every run; the file bar wholly on screen at each cut (at 1,500 px a second it is 87 px from the top when read is cut in); after scrolling back up, state `empty` and the stub on its paper face; down again, `read`.
- **The four other receipts** (report, old files, token, plans) stay, and each now changes once, on a clock. With script and motion each starts at `sent` (a `.w-sent` word was added to those four file bars). `receipts()` runs on the scroll frame: on the first frame the receipt line is wholly on screen (top at or below 52 px, bottom at or above the screen's bottom edge) a 260 ms timer starts; it cuts the message to `read` and writes the minute. It stays read. A receipt already on or above the screen when the page opens, or gone past the top unseen, is cut to read at once. Reduced motion and no script: read from the first frame. Measured (`tools/rb3_receipts.js`): 266 to 271 ms from that first frame to the cut at 600 and 1,500 px a second (390) and 800 (1280), the receipt on screen at the cut in all twelve cases, one grey tick before and two blue after.
- I chose "each cuts once" over "remove three" because an outgoing message without a receipt stops being a message in this vernacular, and because the plans' receipt being read is what hands him the last line, "轮到你回了". The cost is named under weaknesses (the same small cut seen four times).
- **Frames:** `rebuild3_ticks_390zh_a.png` (in flight; sent at 11 to 42 ms; delivered at 262 to 312; read at 543 to 576, each time printed on the frame), `rebuild3_ticks_390zh_b.png` (back above the drop: empty), `rebuild3_ticks_1280en_a.png`; `rebuild3_story390zh_a.png`, `_b.png`.

### 2. Streak heads (`addSmear()`, `.smear .hd`, `hanger()`)

- `addSmear()` was rewritten. There is no slab. `.hd` is one `clip-path` polygon made of separate four-cornered tines (`quad()`), 5 to 12 per bubble, widths drawn from `WD=[1,6,2,9.5,3.5,1.4,7.5,2.5,5,8]` % of the bubble times 0.75 to 1.25 and never under 2 px (measured 1.9 to 21.9 px at 390, 1.9 to 28.5 px at 1280), 56% of them starting on the bubble's leading edge and the rest 0.3 to 2.5em behind it, each cut off on a slant at its own length. A scrap of the leading edge joins two neighbours only when both start on that edge and a 40% draw says so: 0 to 4 per bubble, 0.5 to 1.1em deep (the verdict's cap was 1.2em). Bars: the same three to five, widths 0.26, 0.34, 0.44, 0.6 and 0.85em (the widest was 1em), lengths 7.6 to 18.6em; two or three per smear now start 2 to 4em ahead of the leading edge (`top:-2em` to `-4em`).
- **A still subject from the first step.** The verdict's own sentence was that between 200 and 530 ms "there is no still subject". The first hanging message ("抬头写 Lumen Events Sdn Bhd") is now cut in on the first step of the run (`hanger(..., 0, 1, T1)`, was `T1-260`) and hangs there, sharp, until its catch at 760 ms. It is the *Night Mail* pouch, and it means one thing is still and readable in every frame of the run.
- **Kept:** the step rate (22 steps over 1,839 ms), the 200 ms hold, `CUTIN=300`.
- **Flash**, by the critic's own script (`tools/c3_flash.py`, 146 lossless frames), run on the v3 file and on this one on this machine. Pixels with more than three flashes in a second, share of the worst 341 x 256 box: 390 Chinese 0.3% (v3 0.3%), 390 English 0.1% (v3 0.7%), 1280 0.7% (v3 0.4%). ## 4. Weak or unverified

1. **The streak is still judged from frames.** Frozen, a smear is now a loose row of tapering strokes with no body and no level edge, and one message hangs still from the first step. Whether twelve steps a second read as speed in the hand, nobody has watched.
2. **Flash moved between conditions.** By the critic's count 1280 went from 0.4% to 0.7% while 390 English went from 0.7% to 0.1%. The worst case is unchanged at 0.7% and the limit is 25%, but "not raised" is true of the maximum, not of every condition.
3. **The receipt's clock is 520 ms long.** That is the verdict's figure; it is also 100 ms over the 420 ms the fourth design principle gives a clocked move. No single move in it is longer than the 177 ms drop.
4. **Four receipts make the same cut.** Each does it once, on its own clock, while its line is on screen. It is still one small move seen four times down the page, and a reader could call that a pattern.
5. **Without script the switch changes the strings and nothing else**: `lang`, the title and the description stay Chinese, the place is not held, and it needs `:has()`.
6. **Margin notes are placed by measured px values** in five `--rows` rules. They were checked at four widths in both languages with the web fonts loaded. With a fallback face or a much larger text size a track grows and the notes below it stand lower than their things; nothing overlaps. In `#story` at 1280 the middle note has about 480 px of empty margin under it, because the leaf is tall and only three things are said about it.
7. **The lane moved to the right.** The verdict suggested moving the stage to the lane's side; I moved the lane to the stage's side instead, because the stage's thread only shows the incoming mess when the sheet is on its right. The critic closed "480 px lane at x 80 to 560" in cycle 3; it is now at x 720 to 1,200.
8. **At 320 px** stage text computes under 11 px and the month columns are 41 px wide, as in every earlier build. Not addressed here.
9. **Chromium only.** `:has()`, `display: contents`, `clip-path` polygons with `calc()` and a visible child in a hidden parent were not tried in Safari or Firefox.
10. **Contrast was not re-sampled.** No colour changed (the same 33 hex values); the unread receipt is `--soft` on the outgoing green, 5.7 to 1 computed.
11. **Hover and focus were sampled, and one focus frame saved, not reopened** after the last run.
